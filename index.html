    Hover or tap a boat for her figures, or a brass dot for that man's boats and ratings. Scroll or pinch to zoom in.

    Status: every commissioning claim for the cohort has been verified against its boat's primary source and is recorded as documented fact. Biographical research on each man is under way. Rosters are Reports of Changes and muster rolls, so officers are not counted.

    Presence on a terminal muster is not a death record. Flier had survivors, and several musters close days or weeks before the loss. Dorado's list is the On Eternal Patrol casualty roster checked against her commissioning roster.

    Keel, launch and commissioning dates follow DANFS as compiled on Wikipedia. Bonefish and Redfin use those commissioning dates rather than the dates in the roster transcriptions.
