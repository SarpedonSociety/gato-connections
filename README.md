# Gato Plankowner Network

An animated ring of all 77 Gato-class submarines, from keel laying through commissioning and loss. Each dot is one enlisted man on a boat's commissioning roster; brass lines follow the 190 men who helped commission more than one Gato.

Part of the Sarpedon Society's Gato-class prosopography. Served at sarpedon.org/gato-connections.

## What this page contains

This public build carries no names and no service numbers. Shared plankowners are identified only by their cohort ID (GAT-001 to GAT-192), with their boats, dates and ratings. The source rosters are kept privately and are not part of this repository.

Every commissioning claim for the cohort has been verified against its boat's primary source. Biographical research on each man is under way.

## Sources

- Commissioning rosters and terminal musters: Reports of Changes and muster rolls, transcribed by the Sarpedon Society.
- Dorado's loss list: the On Eternal Patrol casualty roster, checked against her commissioning roster.
- Keel, launch and commissioning dates: DANFS.
